🧠 Multimodal Forensic Architecture

This system utilizes a dual-stream hybrid architecture to dissect images on a mathematical level:

  • 1. EfficientNet-B3 Backbone: Scans spatial semantics and geometric structures for logical inconsistencies.
  • 2. CBAM Attention Mechanism: Forces the AI to focus on blending boundaries (e.g., neck, hair, lips).
  • 3. FFT Spectrum Analysis: Converts the image to the frequency domain to detect synthetic AI checkerboard artifacts (Diffusion/GANs).
  • 4. SRM Filter Bank: Extracts high-frequency camera noise to identify pixel-level splicing and face-swapping.
